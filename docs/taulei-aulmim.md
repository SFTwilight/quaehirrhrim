Auguries of Innocence, opening stanza
=====================

«᙮ᑖ᙮ᓓᐤᐧ᙮ᐋᓪ᙮ᒦᒻ»

ᐁᕐᐧ᙮ᕌᐤᒻ ᐊᓐᐧ᙮ᐸᒃᐧᐃᓪᐲᒻ ᐋᓓᒻ  
ᒉᐧᐁᕐᐧ᙮ᑌᐤ᙮ᒦᕐ ᐊᓐᐧ᙮ᓴᐃ᙮ᓘᔥ ᙯᒻ  
᙮ᕌᐤᕖᕐ ᐊᐧᑌᕐᒋᐧᑭᑦ ᐆᑌᒻ  
ᒉᐧᐊᓐᐧ᙮ᐃᒃ᙮ᑕᒃ ᙮ᕌᐤᓛᐤᓐᐧᐆᔅᕂᒻ.

«TauLeĩ'AulMĩm»

er'Raaum an'Pah'ilbĩm aulem  
che'er'TeiMĩr an'SaiLush qem,  
Raaufĩr a'terchi'hit utem  
che'an'IhTah Raaulaaun'usrem.

Translated from [William Blake's original text](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/43650/auguries-of-innocence):

To see a World in a grain of Sand  
And a Heaven in a wild Flower,  
Hold Infinity in the palm of your hand  
And Eternity in an Hour.
